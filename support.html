To help us fix a problem quickly, please include your device model, iOS version, the level number and what happened.

How to play

  • Tap a bus that isn't blocked to send it to the stop.
  • Passengers at the front of the line board a bus of their own color, in line order. Four seats fill and the bus drives off.
  • Parking bays are limited. If every bay is full and no parked bus can take the next passenger, the level is lost, so plan which bus goes first.
  • Frozen buses thaw after a few moves. Mystery buses show their color once their way out is clear.

Frequently asked questions

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap Hint to see a good next move. You can also restart the level at any time with the restart button.

How do I restore "Remove Ads" on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, open the game, tap the gear button and choose Restore. Purchases are handled by Apple; for billing or refund requests, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

What does "Remove Ads" change?

It turns off banner ads and automatic full-screen ads. Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one for a hint.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Progress and settings are saved on your device only, so deleting the game removes them.

How do I change my privacy or tracking choices?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open the gear menu and choose Privacy choices. On iOS you can change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Yes. Tap the gear button to open the settings and use the switches.
